FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

A few quick answers about VA Claim Compass and how app data is handled.

Does VA Claim Compass submit VA claims?

No. Claim Compass is a planning and estimation tool only. It does not submit claims or interact with VA systems.

Are compensation estimates official?

No. Estimates are based on publicly available VA compensation tables and rating rules, but they are not official determinations.

Is my data shared with anyone?

No. App data is not transmitted to external servers unless explicitly disclosed.
