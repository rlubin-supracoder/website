VA Claim Compass

VA Claim Compass helps veterans and service members estimate VA disability compensation scenarios for planning purposes.

  • Uses publicly available compensation tables and rating logic
  • Does not submit claims or connect to VA systems
  • Stores claim scenario data locally on the device
